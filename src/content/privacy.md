## 1. Who we are

Fable Plus operates a marketplace and business-management platform connecting Customers with independent Providers. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, how we protect it, and your rights.

## 2. The data we collect

- **Account & contact:** name, phone number (verified by OTP), email, password (Providers/staff), profile details.
- **Customer service data:** vehicles (incl. number plate where you provide it), addresses, booking history, preferences (My Garage).
- **Provider verification data:** identity-verification (KYC) information, tax details (legal name, address, DOB/company number, NI/UTR, bank reference) as required by HMRC platform-reporting rules, insurance status, business details.
- **Transaction data:** bookings, payments (processed by Stripe — we don't store card numbers), payouts, tips, disputes.
- **Content:** photos (incl. before/after "Reveals"), reviews, messages.
- **Technical:** device, app usage, approximate location (for "near me"), cookies/identifiers.
- **Communications:** support contacts, notification preferences and consents.

## 3. Why we use it (purposes & lawful basis)

| Purpose | Lawful basis |
|---|---|
| Provide bookings, payments, accounts | Contract |
| Provider verification & tax reporting | Legal obligation (HMRC), contract |
| Safety, fraud prevention, security | Legitimate interests, legal obligation |
| Service notifications (transactional) | Contract |
| Marketing notifications & rebook nudges | **Consent** (opt-in, withdrawable) |
| Improve the platform & AI features (your own data) | Legitimate interests / contract |
| Cross-provider AI learning (aggregated) | **Consent** (where applicable) |
| Special-category data (e.g. biometrics, if used) | **Explicit consent** |

## 4. AI and your data

- AI features (concierge, copilot, summaries, voice) are **grounded in your own data** and produce suggestions, not automated decisions with legal/significant effect without human involvement.
- We **do not** feed special-category or another user's identifiable data into AI.
- Any AI that learns across providers uses **aggregated/anonymised** data and, where required, **consent** (FW32).

## 5. Who we share it with

- **Payment processing:** Stripe (handles card data and payouts).
- **Identity verification:** [Stripe Identity / Onfido].
- **The other party to a booking:** a Customer's necessary details are shared with the **Provider they book** (and vice versa) — never with other customers or other providers. Calls are **masked** to protect phone numbers.
- **Service providers:** hosting, analytics, communications — under data-processing agreements.
- **Authorities:** where legally required (incl. HMRC reporting).
- We **do not sell** your personal data, and we do not run third-party ads that pay to target you.

## 6. How we protect it

Server-side tenant isolation so no customer sees another's data; encryption in transit and at rest; KYC/identity data access-restricted and encrypted; secrets vaulted; 2FA for Providers/staff; audit logging; OWASP/Cyber Essentials practices (FW34).

## 8. Your rights

You have the right to **access, correct, delete (erasure), restrict, object, and port** your data, and to **withdraw consent** for marketing or other consent-based processing at any time. Deletion requests are honoured across all our systems (database, media, analytics, any biometric data). To exercise rights, contact [privacy@…]. You may also complain to the **ICO** (ico.org.uk).

## 9. Marketing & notifications

You have two notification sets: **mandatory transactional** (needed to use the service) and **optional marketing** (off by default, opt-in, with one-tap unsubscribe). Marketing consent is separate from transactional and freely withdrawable (FW33).
