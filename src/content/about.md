# Fable Plus — About Us

**In-app "About" content · Customer- and provider-facing · The story, the mission, the values**

---

## Who we are

Fable Plus is a UK platform that connects people who want their car cleaned with independent, local valeting businesses — and gives those businesses the tools to run everything in one place. We started with mobile car care, but we're building the operating system for mobile home services more broadly.

## What makes us different

Most platforms in this space take a cut of every job a provider does. **We don't.** Providers pay one simple subscription, and they keep 100% of what they earn — whether the customer came from our marketplace or from their own book. For customers, booking is free: no platform fees, ever, and you can book in seconds without even creating an account.

We believe the people doing the work should keep the rewards of it. That single principle shapes everything — our pricing, our tools, and the way we treat the providers who make the platform what it is.

## What you can do here

**If you're a customer:** find trusted, verified local valeters, see real before-and-after work and genuine reviews, book to your door in a few taps, pay securely, and rebook in one tap when your car's due. Every booking with a verified provider is backed by our Guarantee.

**If you're a provider:** run your whole business in one app — your own clients and new customers from our marketplace, your calendar, payments, your team, and a storefront that looks like *your* brand. Smart tools and AI help you fill quiet days, win back customers, and see exactly how your business is doing — without ever taking a slice of your earnings.

## Our Guarantee

When you book a verified provider and pay through Fable Plus, you're covered. If a clean doesn't meet a reasonable standard, you can raise it and we'll help put it right. Trust is the product — the before-and-after, the verified badge, the reviews, and the Guarantee all exist so you can book a stranger with confidence.

## The bigger vision

Car washing is the start. The same platform — the bookings, the payments, the trust, the tools — is built to carry gardening, exterior cleaning, and other mobile home services. One place to find trusted local help, and one place for independent businesses to grow, on fair terms.

## Our values

- **Fair to the people doing the work.** No commission on the job. The provider keeps what they earn.
- **Trust you can see.** Verified providers, real evidence, honest reviews, a real Guarantee.
- **Simple and transparent.** One subscription for providers, free booking for customers, no hidden fees.
- **Local and independent.** We help small businesses thrive, not replace them.
- **Privacy and safety first.** Your details stay between you and your provider, protected and never sold.
