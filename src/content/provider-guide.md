# Fable Plus — Provider Guide (How the App Works)

**Every provider-facing section, explained · A functional walkthrough for the build team and for in-app help**

> What each part of the provider experience does and how it functions, section by section — so the build team constructs each flow and the help team can explain it. Pairs with FW27 (control plane), FW28 (payments), FW29 (storefront), FW30 (verification), FW31–33 (AI, notifications).

---

## 1. Signing up & onboarding
1. Register with your **phone number** (OTP) — no password wall to get in.
2. Add **business basics:** name, service area (postcode + radius), services (from the catalogue, pre-priced, editable).
3. **Pick a plan:** Solo / Growth / Fleet (start on Solo, upgrade any time; Founding offer if eligible).
4. Land in your **dashboard** immediately, with a **go-live checklist** showing what unlocks listing.

## 2. Verification (Verified vs Unverified)
- **Verified (the blue tick):** complete identity verification (KYC), provide tax details (HMRC requirement), set up payouts (Stripe Connect), enable 2FA. Unlocks the **marketplace**, **in-app payments**, the **Guarantee**, and the **badge** all customers see.
- **Unverified (no badge):** use the app as a **management tool for your own clients**, taking payment **in person** only — no marketplace, no in-app payments, no Guarantee. The on-ramp if you're not registered yet. **Upgrade is one tap** when you're ready.
- Each account has a short reference (e.g. `FP-7K2Q`) for support.

## 3. Your dashboard (command-center)
Your home base: today's jobs, expected earnings, new leads, recent reviews, and **AI prompts** ("you turned away 14 jobs — Growth would catch them"). At a glance: how the business is doing, what needs action.

## 4. Calendar & jobs
Your bookings in a calendar, with **travel buffers**, **daylight-aware** slots, and (Fleet) **zone-based routing**. Accept/decline marketplace leads, manage your own client bookings, reschedule, and mark jobs complete. Each job holds the customer, service, address, and a **masked call button**.

## 5. Marketplace leads vs your own clients
- **Marketplace leads:** new customers we send you (counts against your plan's lead allowance — **no commission**).
- **Your own clients (BYOC):** customers you brought (via your link/QR) — **never count as leads, never commissioned**, unlimited bookings on any plan.
The app tags each booking so you always know which is which.

## 6. Bringing your own clients (BYOC) & My QR
- **Import** your existing clients (they don't count against limits until they book/link).
- **Bulk-invite** via a share link; signups auto-link to you.
- **My QR / link:** your personal booking code to share on socials or hand to customers — they book straight into your calendar, commission-free.
- **Recurring schedules** for regulars.

## 7. Services & pricing
Set your services and prices, typically **by vehicle size** (one-tap plate lookup helps customers get the right price). Add requirements (water/power access), durations, and a cancellation policy. You're always in control of your own pricing.

## 8. Your storefront (branding)
Make your profile look like *your* brand (FW29):
- **Free:** logo + one accent colour.
- **Self-serve themer (included in higher plans):** any colours, fonts, hero image, layout template.
- **AI brand studio (higher plans):** describe your brand + upload references → AI builds a full branded storefront (palette, fonts, layout, custom imagery, written copy), refined by chat. Applies instantly.

## 9. Payments
- **Verified:** take payment in-app via Stripe — **cash**, **instant**, or **standard** settlement; tips supported; deposits/no-show protection per your policy. **We take 0% of the job.**
- **Unverified:** payment **in person**, arranged directly with your client; the app can mark a booking "paid (in person)" for your records, but no money flows through us.

## 10. Payroll & team (Fleet)
- **Team/operatives:** add team members (unlimited seats on Fleet) via the same link mechanic; each gets the operative app with their jobs and route.
- **Payroll engine:** the app calculates what you owe your operatives (by job split or rate) and **reports** it for you to pay — it helps you run payroll; it's not your employer.
- **Operative scorecards** and activity tracking.

## 11. Analytics & your activity
See jobs, revenue, tips, new vs repeat customers, and your **subscription cost as a % of revenue** (so the value is obvious). A **GitHub-style activity heatmap** shows your consistency. Forecasts (Fleet) project upcoming demand by area.

## 12. AI tools
- **Plain-language questions:** "How much did I make last month?" "Who are my best repeat clients?" — answered from your own data.
- **Summaries:** weekly/fortnightly/monthly digests, review summaries, client snapshots, season recaps (you choose the cadence).
- **Recommendations:** pricing tips, quiet-day prompts, "time to add an operative."
- **Voice AI receptionist (Fleet):** answers your calls, quotes from your prices, books into your calendar, hands off on anything unusual.
- The AI **only suggests — it never moves your money** — and you can override or switch off anything.

## 13. Notifications
- **Essential** booking alerts (always on).
- **Optional** marketing/rebook prompts to your customers — **you approve each** (default) or switch on **autonomous** sending; only reaches customers who've opted in.
- Set your **summary cadence** and **rebook-nudge mode** in settings.

## 14. B2B (Fleet)
Manage business accounts (dealerships, rental, lease-return, taxi/PHV): multi-site, agreed **rate cards**, **net-30 invoicing**, and a **per-vehicle QC/evidence dashboard** that's the selling point. A separate platform fee applies to B2B (the one place a fee exists).

## 15. Plans & upgrading
Start where you fit; the app shows you when you're hitting your **lead/client/seat** limits and what the next tier unlocks ("you used all 20 leads — Growth gives you 90"). Upgrade/downgrade any time; annual billing available.

## 16. Support
In-app help and a real person for disputes, Guarantee claims, verification questions, and anything else — with your job context attached.

---

## The provider journey, in order
Sign up → (optionally) verify for the marketplace and payments → import your own clients and share your QR → set services, prices, and your branded storefront → take jobs (your own + marketplace leads) → get paid (0% commission) → manage your team and payroll (Fleet) → let AI summarise, advise, and answer your calls → upgrade as you grow.
