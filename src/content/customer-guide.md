# Fable Plus — Customer Guide (How the App Works)

**Every customer-facing section, explained · A functional walkthrough for the build team and for in-app help**

> This describes what each part of the customer experience does and how it should function, section by section — so the build team knows exactly what to construct and the help team can explain it. Pairs with the architecture specs (FW26, FW30) and the screenshots referenced in the session.

---

## 1. Home ("For You")
The landing screen after opening the app. Shows the customer's **upcoming booking** (with time, price, service, and quick actions: get directions, add to calendar), a **"Book again"** row of recent providers, and contextual prompts (gift cards, seasonal offers). Personalised, photo-led, clean (The Gleam shell). Bottom navigation: Home, Search, Bookings, Profile.

## 2. Search & discovery
- **Map + list** of nearby providers (like the venue map in the references), with ratings and distance.
- **Filters & sort:** service type, price, availability, rating, verified-only.
- **Natural-language search** (AI): "mobile valet near me Saturday morning under £60 with great reviews."
- Only **verified providers** appear in marketplace search. Each result shows photos, rating, distance, and "from £X."

## 3. The provider storefront
The provider's branded page (themed per FW29 — their logo, colours, imagery). Shows their services and prices (by vehicle size), portfolio of before/after **Reveals**, reviews, ratings, location/area, and a clear **Book** button. This is where branding makes each provider feel distinct.

## 4. Booking (guest or account)
1. Pick a service and time slot (real availability from the provider's calendar).
2. Add your **vehicle** (one-tap number-plate lookup auto-fills make/model/size; or manual) and **address**.
3. See the full summary: price, what's included, duration, cancellation policy.
4. **Guest:** verify your phone by OTP (no password, no account wall). **Account holder:** confirm.
5. Pay or authorise (verified providers) — or arrange in person (unverified providers).
6. Booked. An account is auto-created (claimable in one tap) so your details and history are saved.

## 5. The day of the wash
- **Reminders** to both you and the provider ahead of time.
- **Live status / en-route tracking** and **"Getting there"** info so you know when they'll arrive (pre-empts "where are they?").
- **Venue/booking details**, add-to-calendar, and a **masked call button** to reach the provider without exchanging personal numbers.

## 6. The Reveal, reviews & tips
After the job, see the **before/after Reveal** — the satisfying moment — which you can **share**. Leave a **review** and **photos**, and add a **tip** if you'd like. Reviews are tied to real bookings (genuine only).

## 7. Rebooking & Wash Plans
- **One-tap rebook** of your last wash, any time.
- **Smart reminders** (if opted in): we nudge you when your car's likely due, timed to your pattern, the weather, and the season.
- **Wash Plans:** subscribe to regular washes with your chosen provider at a discount, auto-scheduled — pause, skip, or change provider freely.

## 8. My Garage
Your vehicles in one place, with their **wash and protection history** (e.g. "ceramic applied March — top-up due"), across every provider you've used. The concierge reads this to recommend the right service. The more history, the smarter the suggestions.

## 9. Gift cards & wallet
- **Send a gift card** (a wash as a gift) — popular at Christmas, redeemable any time.
- **Wallet:** your balance of credit (from referrals, gifts, or goodwill). Promotional credit only.
- **Referrals:** invite friends for rewards once their booking completes.

## 10. Favourites & profile
- **Favourites:** save providers you like for quick rebooking.
- **Profile:** your details, vehicles, addresses, and **wallet balance**.

## 11. Settings
Manage **notifications** (essential booking alerts always on; optional offers/reminders opt-in with one-tap unsubscribe), **social logins**, **language**, **password** (if set), **privacy policy / terms**, and **delete account** (removes your data across our systems).

## 12. Support
In-app help center (this guide's FAQ), and a route to a **real person** for anything unresolved — including Guarantee claims and disputes, reviewed with the job's evidence.

---

## The customer journey, in order
Discover → view a branded storefront → book in seconds (guest or account, vehicle + address) → get reminders and track on the day → enjoy the Reveal, review and tip → rebook in one tap or set a Wash Plan → build My Garage history → refer friends. Free to book, covered by the Guarantee with verified providers, details kept private throughout.

*Customer guide in one line: find a trusted, verified, branded local valeter; book to your door in a few taps without an account; know exactly when they'll arrive; love the before-and-after; and rebook in one tap — all free of platform fees and backed by a guarantee.*
