## For Customers

### Booking
**Do I need an account to book?** No. You can book as a guest — you just verify your phone number so your provider can reach you and confirm. We'll create an account for you afterwards that you can claim in one tap to track bookings and rebook faster.

**How do I book?** Find a provider (search, a provider's link, or browse), pick a service and a time, add your vehicle and address, and confirm. You'll see the price, what's included, how long it takes, and the cancellation policy before you pay.

**How does pricing work?** Providers set their own prices, often by vehicle size. You'll always see "from £X" and a total before you confirm. **Customers never pay a platform fee.**

**Can I rebook easily?** Yes — one tap to rebook your last wash, and we'll remind you when your car's likely due (only if you've opted into reminders).

**What are Wash Plans?** A subscription with your chosen provider (e.g. every 2–4 weeks) at a discount, booked automatically. Pause, skip, or change any time.

### Payments & cancellation
**How do I pay?** Securely in the app for verified providers (handled by Stripe). Some providers (unverified) take payment in person — you'll see this before booking.

**Can I cancel or reschedule?** Yes, per the provider's cancellation policy, shown before you book and in your booking details.

**What if I'm not happy with the clean?** If you booked a **verified** provider and paid in-app, our **Guarantee** applies — raise it within [24 hours] with photos and we'll help put it right (re-clean or credit). This is in addition to your legal rights.

**What about damage?** Damage is handled separately from the Guarantee, through our dispute process and the provider's insurance — contact support.

### Trust & safety
**What does the blue "Verified" tick mean?** That provider has completed full identity and business verification. Verified providers appear on our marketplace, take secure in-app payments, and carry the Guarantee.

**Will my details be shared?** Only with the provider you book — never with other customers or other providers. Calls go through a masked number so your phone number stays private. We never sell your data.

### Account & notifications
**What notifications will I get?** Essential booking updates (you can't turn these off — you need them), and optional offers/reminders (off by default, opt in any time, unsubscribe in one tap).

**How do I delete my account or data?** In Settings → Delete account, or contact support. We delete your data across our systems.

## For Providers

### Getting started
**How do I join?** Sign up with your phone number, pick a plan, and add your services. To take in-app payments and appear on the marketplace, complete verification.

**What's the difference between Verified and Unverified?** **Verified** (full ID + tax details + payout setup + 2FA) gets you the blue tick, the marketplace, in-app payments, and the Guarantee. **Unverified** lets you manage your own clients and take payment in person, with no marketplace or in-app payments — a great way to start if you're not registered yet. Upgrade any time.

**Why do you ask for tax details?** UK rules require platforms to collect and report seller tax information. We collect it at verification, and you get your own copy each year to help with Self-Assessment.

### Plans & fees
**How much do you take from my jobs?** **Nothing.** Zero commission. You pay one monthly subscription and keep 100% of what you earn — including customers we send you.

**What are the plans?** Three tiers — Solo, Growth, and Fleet — differing by how many marketplace leads, client slots, and team seats you get, and which tools and AI are included. Start where you fit; the app tells you when upgrading would pay off.

**Is there a limit on bookings?** No limit on bookings from your **own** clients — book as many as you like on any plan. What scales by tier is **marketplace leads** (new customers we send you), client slots, and team seats.

### Running your business
**Can I bring my own clients?** Yes — import them and share your own booking link/QR. They book through you, and we take no commission. Your own clients never count as marketplace leads.

**Can I make my profile match my brand?** Yes. A free basic version (logo + colour) for everyone; a self-serve themer; and an AI brand studio that builds a fully branded storefront from your description and references — included in higher plans.

**How do I get paid?** Verified providers are paid out via Stripe (cash, instant, or standard settlement). Unverified providers take payment in person.

**What help do the AI tools give me?** Plain-language answers about your business, weekly/monthly summaries, review summaries, pricing tips, quiet-day prompts, and (on Fleet) a Voice AI receptionist that answers calls and books jobs. The AI only ever **suggests** — it never moves your money — and you're always in control.

**Can I call my customers?** Yes — from the booking, tap to call. The call connects through a masked number so neither of you exposes your personal mobile.

### Support & disputes
**How do I get help or handle a dispute?** Contact support in-app. Disputes and Guarantee claims are reviewed by our team with the evidence from the job.
